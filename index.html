FAQs

Good to know.

Does the customer need an app?

Usually no. A compatible NFC-enabled phone can open a programmed link. Customers can also scan the QR code if NFC is unavailable or switched off.

Can I add my own logo and link?

Yes. Share your logo, business details and the destination URL when ordering. Final customisation depends on the selected product.

Will the stand automatically increase reviews?

The stand makes it easier to reach your review page, but reviews are written by customers voluntarily. No product can guarantee a specific number of reviews.
